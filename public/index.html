LEARN THE SIGNALS

Know what a check can tell you.

Short explainers and a quick quiz help turn a report into useful questions.

01 / CONNECTION

TLS validates a connection

A valid certificate lets the scanner verify the certificate chain and hostname. It does not show what the site does with information after delivery.

02 / HEADERS

Headers are one layer

Security headers guide browser behavior. Missing headers merit context and review; they do not prove the site is exploitable.

03 / PRIVACY

Policy text is a statement

Topic matches summarize accessible wording. They cannot confirm whether actual data practices follow that wording.
